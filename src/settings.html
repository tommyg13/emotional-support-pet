Choose your support buddy

Click one to pick it. The next press of the heart button brings it up.

AI chat

Turn AI ON beside Send to chat. Messages go directly to your selected provider using your API account. Keys are encrypted on this computer. Chat history stays in memory until you close the dock or start a new chat.

Search Cat

Enter opens a web search in your browser. Focus it with Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + S. Drag the cat to move it.

Search character

The puppy turns its head, wags its tail, and enjoys a bone treat and a ball. Hold a water or food button to bring it to your companion. All three companions can roam. The panda waddles, carries bamboo, chases a leaf, and settles into a seated doze. The puppy has a four-leg walk and trot. Companions rest when idle. The puppy wakes before moving or playing.

Your cat’s name

Up to 24 characters. Leave empty to use “Your cat”. Saved on this computer.

Cat personality

Choose how lively your cat feels. All personalities work offline. Sleepy rests sooner; Playful is more expressive.

Search Cat breaks

After continuous computer activity, a larger cat appears. Close or snooze it for 15 minutes. Time away from the computer does not count.

Developer tools

Preview moods instantly without waiting for inactivity timers. These controls appear only in developer mode.

Puppy reactions

Select Pomeranian in Search settings and enable the dock first. These buttons skip the idle and away waiting times.

Panda reactions

Select Bamboo panda and enable the search dock first.
